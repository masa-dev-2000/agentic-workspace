# Sales Preflight Checklist

## Internal approval

- Meeting type and desired decision are explicit.
- Representative approved the target price, floor, payment timing, and negotiation authority.
- Current workload and maximum safe client count are known.
- Delivery owner and backup are known.

## Client and problem

- Desired business outcome is stated in the client's language.
- Current workflow, volume, time, errors, and channels are facts or marked unknown.
- Facts, inferences, and hypotheses are visibly separated.
- Decision makers, users, approvers, and data owners are identified.

## Offer

- First paid unit is small enough to deliver and valuable enough to buy.
- Included work, exclusions, deliverables, and acceptance evidence are explicit.
- Later phases are shown without being bundled into the current fee.
- Period, provider effort, client participation, cadence, and approval path are stated.
- External fees, credentials, and third-party dependencies are visible.

## Feasibility and PoC

- Every high-risk capability has a named validation method.
- External integrations use sandbox or authorized live-account evidence where needed.
- Pass/fail criteria and retained evidence are defined.
- Production sending, payment, publishing, and shipment are disabled unless explicitly authorized.

## Close

- The proposal asks for one clear decision.
- Likely objections and factual answers are prepared.
- Estimate and contract can be sent immediately after agreement.
- Every next action has an owner, deadline, and completion signal.
