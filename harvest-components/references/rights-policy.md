# Rights policy

## Accepted source bases

- `personal-owned`: the user owns the source and records evidence.
- `contract-authorized`: a contract or written permission explicitly permits this reuse and is referenced.

Filesystem access, employment, repository membership, public visibility, and absence of a license are not authorization.

## External dependency allowlist

MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, ISC, and 0BSD may pass the automated dependency gate when attribution and notices are preserved. Unknown, custom, source-available, copyleft, or incompatible licenses require human/legal review.

## Always exclude

Secrets, personal/customer data, copyrighted content and assets, brand identity, product wording, proprietary schemas, domain-specific workflows, confidential algorithms, and code whose provenance cannot be established.

## Decision rule

When evidence is missing or ambiguous, record `blocked-rights-review`. Do not convert uncertainty into permission. Registration requires a named evidence reference, clean-room pass, tests, provenance, and explicit human approval.
