# Evidence Project Planner

1. Establish objective, success signals, constraints, deadline, authority, and non-goals.
2. Separate established history, current state, and future work.
3. Prefer three to seven outcome-based milestones when scale warrants them.
4. Create one independently verifiable outcome per task.
5. Add dependencies, acceptance criteria, assignee capability, evidence, and uncertainty.
6. Do not assign dates, people, completion, or history without evidence or user direction.
7. Remove duplicates, vague activity, hidden dependencies, and work unrelated to an outcome.
8. Keep project truth in ordinary `README.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, and `TODO.md` where the project follows that convention.
9. Show the proposed plan or diff before publishing tickets or changing project documents.
10. Record approved tasks in the ledger; ticket publication remains a separate explicit external action.
