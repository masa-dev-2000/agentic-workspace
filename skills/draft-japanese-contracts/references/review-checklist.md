# Contract review checklist

## Deal consistency

- Are scope, exclusions and separately contracted work unambiguous?
- Is the fee fixed or variable? Is any overage rule actually agreed?
- Do the estimate and contract show the same amount and tax treatment?
- Is payment clearly advance or arrears?
- Are term, automatic renewal and non-renewal notice aligned?
- Is the notice channel usable in practice?

## Client-facing quality

- Remove drafting notes, tool names, internal comments and “締結前必須”.
- Prefer plain Japanese and short articles.
- Do not repeat the same operational detail in the estimate and contract.
- Avoid named vendors when the intended obligation is vendor-neutral.
- Preserve mandatory client wording verbatim.

## Party and signature integrity

- Opening sentence defines the full legal parties as 甲 and 乙.
- Signature block repeats the roles and contains known address, legal name and representative.
- Do not leave a known field blank.
- Check spelling, postal code, full-width/half-width characters and representative spacing.

## Approval and evidence

- Identify the approved channel and authorized approver.
- Require explicit text for material approval.
- State whether stamps, reactions or silence count.
- Record oral decisions afterward in the designated channel.
- Never send credentials or API keys through ordinary chat.

## Data and AI

- Limit use to the engagement purpose.
- Prohibit unapproved model training or service improvement use.
- Use approved services only.
- Cover access control, minimum data, incidents, deletion and subcontracting.
- Keep human approval for external communications and regulated decisions.

## Final verification

- Search removed terms globally, including tables and headers.
- Search every mandatory phrase.
- Check contradictions such as auto-renewal versus no auto-renewal.
- Inspect the rendered DOCX when the renderer is available.
- State clearly when only structural QA was possible.
