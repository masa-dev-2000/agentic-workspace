# Pricing and Capacity

## Internal calculation

Use:

`required monthly revenue = fixed costs + delivery payroll + sales/admin costs + risk reserve + retained margin`

Then:

`minimum average revenue per client = required monthly revenue / safely serviceable clients`

Estimate serviceable clients from constrained delivery capacity, not optimism. Include meetings, preparation, implementation, testing, documentation, approvals, rework, and support.

## Fixed-fee design

A monthly fixed fee requires:

- stable objectives;
- bounded work categories;
- explicit exclusions;
- prioritization when requests exceed capacity;
- separate agreement for materially different deliverables;
- a review or termination mechanism.

Do not expose an hourly rate unless the commercial model requires it. Internally, calculate the effective hourly rate to test sustainability.

## Client-facing rationale

Explain:

- the business outcome being advanced;
- the work and evidence included in the period;
- the uncertainty and delivery responsibility being assumed;
- the client's saved coordination or development burden;
- what is intentionally excluded.

Avoid claiming guaranteed savings without baseline data.

## Scope response hierarchy

When budget and desired scope do not match:

1. preserve the outcome;
2. narrow channels or use cases;
3. reduce automation depth;
4. phase external integrations;
5. change the term;
6. only then reconsider price with internal approval.
