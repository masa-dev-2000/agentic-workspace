# Deck architectures

Select one primary architecture. Supporting detail can become an appendix or separate artifact.

## Meeting summary

Use to confirm shared understanding after a discussion.

1. Meeting conclusion
2. Client statements and direct answers
3. Agreed priorities and sequencing
4. Decisions made
5. Open questions: method, owner, resolution date
6. Actions: client, provider, owner, deadline
7. Confirmation requested

Do not turn this into a disguised sales proposal.

## Decision proposal

Use when the client must choose whether or how to proceed.

1. Decision and recommendation
2. Client objective and constraint
3. Future state and why it matters
4. Options, including status quo
5. Evidence and assumptions
6. Business value and scenarios
7. Risks, unknowns, and validation gates
8. Roadmap and client burden
9. Scope, exclusions, price, and terms
10. Requested decision and deadline

## Delivery plan

Use after direction or contract is agreed.

1. Outcomes and acceptance criteria
2. Workstreams and dependencies
3. Milestones and decision gates
4. Client inputs and effort
5. Roles and governance cadence
6. Risks, escalation, and stop conditions
7. Immediate kickoff checklist

## Internal sales memo

Never show this artifact to the client.

1. Desired commercial outcome
2. Decision-makers, influencers, and approval path
3. Client decision criteria and objections
4. Evidence required to close
5. Commercial boundaries and alternatives
6. Risks and likely negotiation paths
7. Owner, follow-up, and deadline

## Transformation or AI proposal

Overlay these questions on the selected primary architecture:

- Which scarce judgment or operational constraint is being addressed?
- Which quality cannot be sacrificed?
- What remains intentionally human-controlled?
- What data, permissions, APIs, and behavior examples are required?
- What can be verified in one week, one month, and later?
- What is the explicit Go/Hold/Rework/No-Go rule?
- What persists after the engagement ends?
- What ongoing costs and maintenance remain?
- What observation would make the proposed solution unnecessary?

## Traceability test

Maintain a working matrix:

| ID | Client statement/question | Answer | Claim type | Evidence | Unknown resolution | Slide |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

A material row may not remain without either a direct supported answer or an explicit test, owner, and resolution date.
